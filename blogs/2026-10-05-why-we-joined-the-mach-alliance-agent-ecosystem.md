The move from single AI-powered assistants to systems of agents that work together is happening as we speak. Those agents don’t all come from one vendor, they come from a CRM you deployed years ago, data platforms you migrated to last year, and AI model providers you’re evaluating today, and even agents your own team built to connect different systems. And getting them to interoperate, to work together safely, efficiently, and compliantly, is a problem the whole industry is facing.
