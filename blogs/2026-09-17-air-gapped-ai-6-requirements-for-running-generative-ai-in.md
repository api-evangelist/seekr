For most enterprises, deploying generative AI means calling a model that lives in someone else’s cloud. For a defense intelligence unit, a classified program, or a critical-infrastructure operator, that is exactly the thing they cannot do. Their data cannot leave the perimeter, and the network has no path to the public internet by design.
